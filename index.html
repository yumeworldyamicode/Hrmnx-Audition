WHO
ARE WE?

Hrmnx Entertainment is a community project that specialises in making musical talents (soloists and groups).
And we can make your dreams come true.

Our organisation donates 25% of our earnings to good cause or charity. We have established a community of 89 people to volunteerly work with us

We decided to make the dreams of many come true by becoming one of the first entertainment companies in the music industry to do free and for all ages online idol-like training.

Our system works the same as any other entertainment's audition system, you apply with an audition video of two categories, singing and dancing (rapping instead of singing is also allowed).

We also have a system for different ages, every system has it's certain levels. You can do this entirely for fun or take this extremely seriously to train for a big entertainment company's acceptance.


Our organisation was founded by Sophia Rodriguez, a young girl from the U.S. who had a dream. She met up with one of our staff, Osana Sasaki, and her cousin, Chiyo Tanaka, to make their dreams come true.

Most of our staff are friends of each other, they work with us volunteerly. Our first project was Harmonia.

Chiyo always dreamt of being an idol, when financial issues were high, she met up with Sophia, and got the role of Harmonia. She had to be an idol for at least 3 months for our first project.

Harmonia went viral, she became known among a niche branch of the K-Pop community. The first project was a success. Then, we started our next project, SEIUN.

We started doing online auditions at first, then we started doing auditions locally in Japan. We came to the conclusion of having three members: Hyejin, Suji and Yennie.

We also started up 2 more artists, Osako's Diary and Ai Hina Sato.


And we're still building the foundation and history of Hrmnx Entertainment. Do you want to be apart of it?

WHAT
IS OUR PLAN?

Hrmnx Entertainment is a community project that is working on creating the project and social media platform "KiKi".
And you can be a part of it.

Our plan is making a new project called KiKi. KiKi will be our new social media platform for people to talk about their favorite idols, DM their favorite idols and most importantly, train to become an idol.

KiKi as a social media platform is only the top of the iceberg, we're creating another system called KiKi Trainees, which is gonna be another platform to take online lessons to learn the base of becoming a decent idol.

Hrmnx Entertainment and KiKi will collaborate to create two platforms, Hrmnx Audition and KiKi Trainees, and together these platforms form the alliance Hrmnx Training & Development.

All KiKi's actual Trainees are handled by HOSHIZORA Co. and Hrmnx Training & Development, they are part of the Hrmnx Idol & Trainees family.


We are busy creating not only KiKi Communication, but also KiKi Trainees under HOSHIZORA Co., but KiKi trainees has a few audition requirements:

To audition you need to be 16+, we are not accepting children under 16 to join us in the idol industry, since the industry is way too harsh for children.

Luckily, we make the industry just a little easier for everyone, we don't do performances or in-real-life meet-ups. Training and idoling should be accessable for everyone.

You can become a trainee under 16 too, the system just works very different and child-friendly. You won't do any auditions, you can just sign up with a kids profile on KiKi.

Becoming a child trainee at Hrmnx Entertainment is still for people above 10 years old. We don't recommend your child to do training like ours under the age of 10, though we are seeking ways to make it accessible for everyone.

Child trainees do less harsh training, singing, dancing and rapping for example are optional things you can choose in your package, though you'll have to choose at least one of them.

OUR
COMMUNITY

Hrmnx Entertainment is a community project that has over 89 staff- and 22 trainee members.
And we are asking you to join us.

Now that all that is aside, let's get to the real part.

Hrmnx Entertainment is a volunteerly powered organisation. We are like an entertainment company, but instead of working for the company's profits, we work for equality and helping people in need.

By joining our community, you agree to work volunteerly, out of your own will and because you want to be a part of funding good cause and charity.

Our community isn't that harsh, everyone has different roles, we have a special work for everyone. Of course, you don't need to be active all the time, there's not enough work for everyone. Whenever there's more work or someone quits, we'll always keep you updated for the most.


Our community has a special platform too, like KiKi, just not owned by HOSHIZORA. The platform NEMAWASHI (or shortly written as NMWS) is the main way how we communicate in a business-like way.

NEMAWASHI is owned by Hrmnx Information Technology or Hrmnx I.T., the part of our organisation that actually makes all the websites and handles technical functions.

We are open to accept anyone in our team, community and everything else beyond that. But first, which parts can I help with?

At Hrmnx Entertainment, you can help us with a lot of things: T&D (Training & Development), A&R (Artist & Repertoire), I.T. (Information Technology), Creative Direction and I&T (Idol & Trainees, under T&D).

There are more things you can help with, but you'll have to learn more to discover them.
