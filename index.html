WHO
ARE WE?

Hrmnx Entertainment is a community project that specialises in making musical talents (soloists and groups).
And we can make your dreams come true.

Our organisation donates 25% of our earnings to good cause or charity. We have established a community of 89 people to volunteerly work with us


We decided to make the dreams of many come true by becoming one of the first entertainment companies in the music industry to do free and for all ages online idol-like training.


Our system works the same as any other entertainment's audition system, you apply with an audition video of two categories, singing and dancing (rapping instead of singing is also allowed).


We also have a system for different ages, every system has it's certain levels. You can do this entirely for fun or take this extremely seriously to train for a big entertainment company's acceptance.
