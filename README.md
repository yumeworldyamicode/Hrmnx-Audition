<h1><mark>WHO</mark> ARE WE?</h1>
<h3>Hrmnx Entertainment is a community project that specialises in <mark>making</mark> musical talents (soloists and groups).
And <mark>we</mark> can make <mark>your</mark> dreams come true.</h3>
<p>Our organisation donates 25% of our earnings to good cause or charity. We have established a community of 89 people to volunteerly work with us We decided to make the dreams of many come true by becoming one of the first entertainment companies in the music industry to do free and for all ages online idol-like training. Our system works the same as any other entertainment's audition system, you apply with an audition video of two categories, singing and dancing (rapping instead of singing is also allowed). We also have a system for different ages, every system has it's certain levels. You can do this entirely for fun or take this extremely seriously to train for a big entertainment company's acceptance.</p>
<p>Our organisation was founded by Sophia Rodriguez, a young girl from the U.S. who had a dream. She met up with one of our staff, Osana Sasaki, and her cousin, Chiyo Tanaka, to make their dreams come true. Most of our staff are friends of each other, they work with us volunteerly. Our first project was Harmonia. Chiyo always dreamt of being an idol, when financial issues were high, she met up with Sophia, and got the role of Harmonia. She had to be an idol for at least 3 months for our first project. Harmonia went viral, she became known among a niche branch of the K-Pop community. The first project was a success. Then, we started our next project, SEIUN. We started doing online auditions at first, then we started doing auditions locally in Japan. We came to the conclusion of having three members: Hyejin, Suji and Yennie. We also started up 2 more artists, Osako's Diary and Ai Hina Sato.</p>
<p>And we're still building the foundation and history of Hrmnx Entertainment. Do <mark>you</mark> want to be apart of it?</p>
